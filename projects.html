RoadRescue

RoadRescue is a web-based vehicle breakdown assistance system designed to help users find nearby mechanics during unexpected vehicle troubles. It uses location-based services to identify available mechanics and allows users to send service requests conveniently. The application enables mechanics to view incoming requests, accept or reject them, and manage assistance based on their availability. It also provides users with a simple way to track their assistance requests and connect with service providers. The main goal of RoadRescue is to make roadside assistance simple, accessible and reliable.
