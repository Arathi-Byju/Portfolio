RoadRescue

A web-based vehicle breakdown assistance system that helps users find nearby mechanics during unexpected vehicle trouble. The goal is to make roadside assistance simple, accessible and reliable.

  • Finds available mechanics nearby using location-based services
  • Lets users send service requests and track their status
  • Lets mechanics view, accept or reject requests based on availability
